## 🌸 ABOUT ME

*Just a developer creating things that make the internet a little more interesting.*

</div>

Hey! I'm **Tanishq Thakur** 👋

I enjoy turning ideas into projects and making things that are both useful and visually appealing.

Currently, I'm building **Resonance**, a live presence badge service that lets people display their gaming, music, chess, and anime activity directly in their GitHub READMEs and other supported platforms.

I like the intersection of technology, creativity, and beautiful design. This profile is where I'll share what I'm building, what I'm exploring, and what comes next.

- 🌱 Exploring new ideas and building projects.
- 🎧 Creating tools that bring a little personality to the web.
- 🌸 Working on **Resonance**.
- ✨ Always looking for something interesting to build next.
